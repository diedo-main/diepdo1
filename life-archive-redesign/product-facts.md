# Product facts used for the direction round

- **Life Archive** is a private, authenticated Vietnamese archive. Its existing flows are home search, query suggestions and history, searchable/filterable results, article reading, audio playback, personal notes/tags, a mobile notes sheet, version selection, and a safe local-to-cloud notes migration.
- The current source of truth is the running code in `app/page.tsx` and `components/archive/v05/`; this direction round does not change that implementation.
- **Readwise Reader** was verified at https://readwise.io/read on 2026-10-28. Its official page confirms a unified library with full-text search, highlighting/annotations and text-to-speech. It is a workflow reference only, not a visual or feature copy.
