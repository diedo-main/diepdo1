# Life Archive — direction-round design spec

## Product and audience

A single owner returns to personal Vietnamese stories, recordings and reflections. They arrive with a half-remembered phrase, listen and read without distraction, then leave a durable private note.

## Common scope

Each direction demonstrates the existing complete path: home/search, suggestions, result filtering, reading, audio, notes and tags, mobile notes sheet, and the safe migration prompt. The prototypes use existing Vietnamese copy and familiar data shapes; they invent neither social activity nor AI features.

## Non-negotiables

- Dark charcoal, olive and ivory remain the visual language.
- Reading is the primary job; owner status remains private and quiet.
- Body copy is at least 16px; touch targets are at least 44px.
- The proposed UI is desktop-first with an explicit mobile reading/notes state.
- The direction round is a visual prototype only. No production component or data contract is modified.

## Form

The archive contains intimate words that return later. Every direction uses a different archive-native form: a quiet reading room, a book margin/index, or an audio listening room—not a social feed or a generic dashboard.
